# Standing Disclaimer

**This skill provides INFORMATION ONLY. It is not legal advice.**

The `crypto-legal-skill` Claude Code skill is an educational and triage tool for Solana-native founders. It surfaces statutes, regulator guidance, decision trees, checklists, and templates. It does **not**:

- Constitute legal advice in any jurisdiction.
- Create an attorney-client relationship.
- Substitute for licensed counsel.
- Produce binding contract language, opinion letters, or filings.
- Guarantee that any statute, regulation, court decision, or regulator position it cites is current at the time you read it.

## Why this matters

Crypto law moves fast. The U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, the European Securities and Markets Authority (ESMA), Brazil's Comissão de Valores Mobiliários (CVM) and Banco Central do Brasil (BCB), the U.S. Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN), the U.S. Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC), and dozens of other regulators issue new guidance, enforcement actions, and rulemakings every month. Court decisions reframe entire doctrines (Howey, Reves, MiCA Title III/IV classification) with each new opinion.

This skill is **calendar-pinned**: each release reflects the regulatory landscape as of a specific month (`metadata.version: "2026-06"` for the current release). Anything you read here may be stale by the time you act on it.

## What to do instead of relying on this skill

1. **Retain crypto-licensed counsel in your operating and target jurisdictions** before launching a token, structuring an entity, signing a binding agreement, responding to a regulator, or making any decision with regulatory consequences.
2. Treat this skill's output as **research-stage material** to brief your counsel — not as a conclusion.
3. Independently verify every statutory citation against the **primary source** (the official statute, regulation, or guidance document) at the date you read it.
4. **Do not act** on output flagged with confidence below `HIGH` without counsel review. Even `HIGH`-confidence output is informational only.

## Hard stops — when the skill will refuse

The skill will decline to proceed without first recommending counsel for:

- **Sanctions / OFAC exposure.** Any question that implies dealing with sanctioned persons, jurisdictions, or entities triggers an immediate escalation.
- **Criminal exposure.** Money-laundering, fraud, securities fraud, tax evasion, unauthorized practice of finance — all hard-stop topics.
- **Live-token securities classification.** "Is my already-launched token a security?" is a question only counsel can answer responsibly.
- **Formal regulator contact.** Subpoenas, Wells notices, CVM ofícios, BaFin inquiries, ANPD investigations.
- **M&A, IPO/SPO, or material corporate transactions.**
- **Pending litigation.** Anything currently in court or arbitration.
- **Binding contract drafting.** The skill will offer checklists and clause libraries but will not draft binding contract language without explicit acknowledgment that you are working with counsel who will review.

## Jurisdictional scope

**Primary (HIGH confidence target):** United States, European Union, Brazil.

**Summary-only (STUB confidence):** All other jurisdictions. The skill will provide general orientation only and tell you to retain local counsel.

If your situation involves a jurisdiction outside the primary set, the skill will say so and refuse to give jurisdiction-specific guidance.
