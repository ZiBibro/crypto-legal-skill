## Primary sources — United States

### Federal statutes + regulations
- **Securities Act of 1933 + Securities Exchange Act of 1934** — US Code, Titles 15 §§77a et seq. and §§78a et seq. (uscode.house.gov)
- **Bank Secrecy Act (31 USC §5311 et seq.)** — uscode.house.gov
- **Internal Revenue Code (26 USC)** — uscode.house.gov
- **Code of Federal Regulations** — ecfr.gov

### Federal regulators
- **SEC** — sec.gov; FinHub crypto framework: sec.gov/finhub
- **CFTC** — cftc.gov
- **FinCEN** — fincen.gov; CVC guidance archives
- **IRS** — irs.gov; Digital Assets page: irs.gov/businesses/digital-assets
- **OFAC** — home.treasury.gov/policy-issues/financial-sanctions
- **FTC** — ftc.gov
- **CFPB** — consumerfinance.gov
- **OCC** — occ.gov

### State regulators (highest-impact)
- **NYDFS** — dfs.ny.gov; BitLicense + Trust Charter
- **California DFPI** — dfpi.ca.gov; DFAL implementation
- **Texas Department of Banking + State Securities Board** — banking.state.tx.us, ssb.texas.gov
- **Wyoming Division of Banking + Secretary of State** — wyomingbankingdivision.wyo.gov, sos.wyo.gov

### US case law access
- **CourtListener** — courtlistener.com (free)
- **Justia** — justia.com (free)
- **Public Access to Court Electronic Records (PACER)** — pacer.uscourts.gov

## Primary sources — European Union

### EU law
- **EUR-Lex** — eur-lex.europa.eu; canonical access to OJ-published EU regulations + directives
- **Specific instruments:**
  - MiCA (Regulation (EU) 2023/1114) — search EUR-Lex
  - GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) — search EUR-Lex
  - TFR (Regulation (EU) 2023/1113) — search EUR-Lex
  - AMLR (Regulation (EU) 2024/1624) — search EUR-Lex
  - AMLA (Regulation (EU) 2024/1620) — search EUR-Lex
  - AMLD6 (Directive (EU) 2024/1640) — search EUR-Lex
  - DAC8 (Council Directive (EU) 2023/2226) — search EUR-Lex
  - MiFID II (Directive 2014/65/EU) — search EUR-Lex

### EU authorities
- **ESMA** — esma.europa.eu; MiCA RTS + ITS publications
- **EBA** — eba.europa.eu; MiCA EMT-related RTS
- **EDPB** — edpb.europa.eu; GDPR guidelines including Guidelines 02/2025 on blockchain (adopted 8 Apr 2025)
- **AMLA** — amla.europa.eu (when operational)

### EU NCAs (national competent authorities — highest-relevance)
- **BaFin** (Germany) — bafin.de
- **AMF** (France) — amf-france.org
- **AFM** (Netherlands) — afm.nl + DNB — dnb.nl
- **CONSOB** (Italy) — consob.it + Banca d'Italia — bancaditalia.it
- **CNMV** (Spain) — cnmv.es + Banco de España — bde.es
- **CSSF** (Luxembourg) — cssf.lu
- **CySEC** (Cyprus) — cysec.gov.cy
- **MFSA** (Malta) — mfsa.mt
- **Central Bank of Ireland** — centralbank.ie

### EU case law
- **CJEU (Court of Justice of the EU)** — curia.europa.eu

## Primary sources — Brazil

### Federal statutes + regulations
- **Planalto** — planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis (canonical Brazilian federal law text)
- **Specific instruments:**
  - Lei nº 14.478/2022 (Marco Legal das Criptomoedas) — planalto.gov.br
  - Lei nº 13.709/2018 (LGPD) — planalto.gov.br
  - Lei nº 6.385/1976 (Lei da CVM) — planalto.gov.br
  - Lei nº 9.613/1998 (Lavagem de Dinheiro) — planalto.gov.br
  - Decreto nº 11.563/2023 (designates BCB as PSAV regulator) — planalto.gov.br

### Brazilian regulators
- **BCB** — bcb.gov.br; Resoluções + Circulares + Comunicados; PSAV regulatory framework via Resoluções 519/520/521 (Nov 2025)
- **CVM** — gov.br/cvm; Pareceres de Orientação + Resoluções including PO 40 and Resolução 175
- **ANPD** — gov.br/anpd; Resoluções including Resolução CD/ANPD 4/2023 (dosimetria)
- **COAF** — gov.br/coaf; Resoluções + reporting frameworks
- **Receita Federal** — gov.br/receitafederal; Instruções Normativas including IN 1888/2019 and IN 2178/2024

### Brazilian case law
- **STF (Supremo Tribunal Federal)** — portal.stf.jus.br
- **STJ (Superior Tribunal de Justiça)** — stj.jus.br
