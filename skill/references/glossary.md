# Glossary

A reference map for the terminology used across crypto-legal regimes. The same English word often means different things to different regulators; this glossary cross-references.

**Confidence:** HIGH for definitions; relationships and cross-jurisdictional mapping require counsel for case-specific application.
**Last statutory review:** 2026-06-15.

## A

**AMLA** — Authority for Anti-Money Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism. Regulation (EU) 2024/1620 establishes AMLA as a new EU agency for direct AML supervision of selected high-risk obliged entities, including some CASPs. Operational from 2025; full powers phased to 2027+.

**AMLR** — Anti-Money Laundering Regulation. Regulation (EU) 2024/1624. The single EU AML rulebook replacing fragmented national AML laws. Most provisions applicable from 10 July 2027.

**AMLD6** — Sixth Anti-Money Laundering Directive. Directive (EU) 2024/1640. National-transposition companion to AMLR. Transposition deadline 10 July 2027.

**ANPD** — Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados (Brazil). The data-protection supervisory authority under LGPD.

**ART** — Asset-Referenced Token. MiCA Title III. A crypto-asset that maintains stable value by referencing another value or right, or combination thereof, including one or more official currencies. Authorization + reserve + redemption requirements per Title III.

**ATS** — Alternative Trading System. SEC-registered trading venue under Regulation ATS (17 CFR §§242.300-303). Relevant when a US trading platform handles securities.

## B

**BCB** — Banco Central do Brasil. Designated regulator for the PSAV regime under Lei 14.478/2022 via Decreto 11.563/2023.

**BIS** — Bank for International Settlements (international standards) OR Bureau of Industry and Security (US Department of Commerce — export controls). Context-dependent.

**BSA** — Bank Secrecy Act, 31 USC §5311 et seq. US base statute for AML obligations on financial institutions including MSBs.

## C

**CASP** — Crypto-Asset Service Provider. MiCA Title V. Any legal person whose occupation or business is the professional provision of one or more crypto-asset services to clients.

**CCPA** — California Consumer Privacy Act, Cal. Civ. Code §1798.100 et seq. As amended by CPRA.

**CDC** — Código de Defesa do Consumidor (Brazil). Consumer-protection statute relevant to B2C contract terms.

**CDD** — Customer Due Diligence. AML term for KYC + ongoing monitoring obligations.

**CFTC** — Commodity Futures Trading Commission (US). Regulator for commodities + derivatives. Treats BTC + ETH as commodities for derivatives purposes; growing enforcement focus on DeFi.

**CIP** — Customer Identification Program. BSA-specific term for KYC at onboarding.

**CIM** — Confidential Information Memorandum (private-placement document) or Composite International Monetary Index (rare in this context).

**COAF** — Conselho de Controle de Atividades Financeiras. Brazil's financial-intelligence unit. Receives suspicious-activity reports (COS — Comunicação de Operação Suspeita).

**CPRA** — California Privacy Rights Act. 2020 amendment to CCPA. Effective 2023. Adds sensitive-PI category, right to correct, right to limit use of SPI, etc.

**CPPA** — California Privacy Protection Agency. Regulator for CCPA / CPRA.

**CR-stablecoin / CR token** — colloquial reference to capital-requirement-bearing tokens under MiCA (typically EMT or significant ART).

**CTR** — Currency Transaction Report. BSA filing for cash transactions ≥ $10,000.

**CVM** — Comissão de Valores Mobiliários (Brazil). Securities regulator. Issued Parecer de Orientação 40 (token classification framework).

## D

**DAC8** — Council Directive (EU) 2023/2226. Implements the OECD Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework (CARF) at EU level.

**DAO** — Decentralized Autonomous Organization. Common branding; legal entity status varies by jurisdiction. Wyoming DAO LLC (Wyoming Statute Title 17, Ch. 31) is one formalization; otherwise unincorporated DAOs face general-partnership-style joint liability in many jurisdictions.

**DFPI** — California Department of Financial Protection and Innovation. State-level regulator for crypto money transmitters and DFAL (Digital Financial Assets Law) covered persons.

**DPO** — Data Protection Officer. GDPR Art. 37. Brazilian equivalent: encarregado (LGPD Art. 41).

**DPIA** — Data Protection Impact Assessment. GDPR Art. 35. Brazilian equivalent: RIPD (Relatório de Impacto à Proteção de Dados, LGPD Art. 38).

## E

**EBA** — European Banking Authority. Develops RTS / ITS for banking + EMT (MiCA) topics.

**ECB** — European Central Bank.

**EDPB** — European Data Protection Board. Issues GDPR coordination guidelines including the Guidelines 02/2025 on blockchain (adopted 8 Apr 2025).

**EMT** — E-Money Token. MiCA Title IV. A crypto-asset that purports to maintain a stable value by referencing the value of one official currency. Issued by an authorized EMI or credit institution; reserve segregation + redemption at par.

**EMI** — Electronic Money Institution. EU regulated entity under Directive 2009/110/EC (EMD2) authorized to issue electronic money.

**ESMA** — European Securities and Markets Authority. Develops RTS / ITS for MiCA, MiFID II, prospectus regulation, market abuse.

## F

**FATF** — Financial Action Task Force. Intergovernmental standard-setter for AML. FATF Recommendation 15 covers virtual-asset service providers (VASPs).

**FinCEN** — Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (US Treasury). Administers the BSA + MSB registration regime.

**FIDC** — Fundo de Investimento em Direitos Creditórios. Brazilian credit-rights-backed investment fund. Tokenizable per CVM Resolução 175.

**FIEA** — Financial Instruments and Exchange Act (Japan).

## G

**GDPR** — General Data Protection Regulation. Regulation (EU) 2016/679.

**GPC** — Global Privacy Control. Browser signal indicating opt-out of sale / sharing under CCPA / CPRA.

## H

**Howey** — *SEC v. W.J. Howey Co.*, 328 U.S. 293 (1946). Four-prong test for "investment contract" (a category of "security" under §2(a)(1) of the Securities Act).

## I

**IEEPA** — International Emergency Economic Powers Act, 50 USC §1701 et seq. US base statute for OFAC sanctions.

**IRS** — Internal Revenue Service (US). Tax authority. Notice 2014-21 treats virtual currency as property.

## L

**LGPD** — Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados. Lei nº 13.709/2018 (Brazil). Brazil's general data protection law.

## M

**MiCA** — Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation. Regulation (EU) 2023/1114. Core EU crypto regulation.

**MiFID II** — Markets in Financial Instruments Directive II. Directive 2014/65/EU. EU base framework for financial instruments + investment services; defines "financial instrument" (Annex I, Section C) — the boundary with MiCA.

**MSB** — Money Services Business. US BSA term. 31 CFR §1010.100(ff). Includes crypto exchangers + administrators + money transmitters.

**MTL** — Money Transmitter License. State-level US licensing requirement (varies by state); not preempted by FinCEN MSB registration.

## N

**NCA** — National Competent Authority. Under MiCA, each EU member state designates an NCA (e.g., BaFin in Germany, AMF in France, AFM/DNB in Netherlands, CONSOB/Banca d'Italia in Italy).

**NYDFS** — New York Department of Financial Services. Operates the BitLicense regime (23 NYCRR Part 200).

## O

**OFAC** — Office of Foreign Assets Control (US Treasury). Administers US sanctions.

## P

**PIPA** — Personal Information Protection Act (South Korea).

**PoO 40** — Parecer de Orientação CVM nº 40/2022. Brazilian token classification framework.

**PSAV** — Prestador de Serviços de Ativos Virtuais. Brazilian term for crypto-asset service provider under Lei 14.478/2022.

## R

**Reves** — *Reves v. Ernst & Young*, 494 U.S. 56 (1990). Family-resemblance test for whether a note is a security under the Securities Act.

**RIPD** — Relatório de Impacto à Proteção de Dados. Brazilian equivalent of DPIA (LGPD Art. 38).

**RTS** — Regulatory Technical Standards. EU regulations developed by ESAs (ESMA / EBA / EIOPA) and adopted by the Commission. Legally binding once adopted.

**ITS** — Implementing Technical Standards. Similar mechanism to RTS; different legal basis.

## S

**SAR** — Suspicious Activity Report. BSA filing required of US MSBs within 30 days of detection.

**SDN** — Specially Designated Nationals and Blocked Persons List. Primary OFAC sanctions list.

**SEC** — Securities and Exchange Commission (US).

**Self-hosted wallet** — A wallet whose private keys are controlled by the user, not a third-party custodian. Subject to enhanced due diligence under MiCA TFR for transfers > €1,000.

**SPI** — Sensitive Personal Information (CPRA category, Cal. Civ. Code §1798.140(ae)). Includes SSN, government ID, financial account credentials, precise geolocation, racial/ethnic origin, religious beliefs, union membership, contents of communications, genetic data, biometric data for unique identification, health data, sex life or sexual orientation.

**SSI** — Sectoral Sanctions Identifications List. OFAC list for sectoral sanctions.

**STR** — Suspicious Transaction Report. EU AMLR equivalent of SAR.

## T

**TFR** — Transfer of Funds Regulation. Regulation (EU) 2023/1113. EU Travel-Rule analogue for crypto-asset transfers between CASPs.

**Travel Rule** — Information-sharing requirement for transmittals of funds between financial institutions. US: 31 CFR §1010.410(f). EU: TFR. International origin: FATF Recommendation 16.

## U

**UCTD** — Unfair Contract Terms Directive 93/13/EEC. EU consumer-protection baseline for B2C contracts.

**Utility token** — A token whose primary function is access to a product or service. Status under each regime varies: typically MiCA Title II (whitepaper + notification), not necessarily a US security if economic substance fits, not a Brazilian "valor mobiliário" if non-investment purpose.

## V

**VAUPA** — Virtual Asset User Protection Act (South Korea). Effective July 2024.

**VASP** — Virtual Asset Service Provider. FATF term. Each FATF member implements via local regime; in the US: MSB; in the EU: CASP; in BR: PSAV.

**Valor mobiliário** — Brazilian term for "security" under Lei 6.385/1976 Art. 2º.
