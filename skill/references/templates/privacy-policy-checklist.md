# Privacy Policy — Drafting Checklist

A checklist for a crypto-product Privacy Policy aligned to GDPR Art. 13/14, LGPD Art. 9, CCPA / CPRA disclosure minimums. The skill does **not** draft binding Privacy Policy language; this walks the structure so counsel can fill in jurisdiction-specific terms.

**Confidence:** HIGH for structure; jurisdiction-specific terms require counsel.
**Last statutory review:** 2026-06-15.

## When to use

- Pre-launch privacy disclosure preparation.
- Material change in data flows (new processing activity, new sub-processor, new on-chain commit).
- Cross-border expansion adding new user jurisdictions.
- Periodic refresh (typically annual, or on regulator update).

## Pre-flight

Confirm:

1. **User jurisdictions** — drives required content (GDPR / CCPA / LGPD overlay).
2. **What personal data is processed** — see DPIA-lite map.
3. **Lawful basis matrix** — see DPIA-lite section 3.
4. **Sub-processors** — list + DPAs in place.
5. **On-chain commits** — what (if anything) goes on-chain that is or can be linked to a person.

## Section-by-section checklist

### Section 1 — Identity of Controller / Encarregado / Privacy Contact

- [ ] Legal entity name + registration + registered address (GDPR Art. 13(1)(a) / 14(1)(a); LGPD Art. 9, I).
- [ ] DPO / Encarregado / Privacy Officer contact information (GDPR Art. 13(1)(b) / 14(1)(b); LGPD Art. 41).
- [ ] Representative in EU (if controller / processor not established in EU but processing reaches EU; GDPR Art. 27).
- [ ] CCPA "Notice at Collection" contact information (Cal. Civ. Code §1798.100(a)).

### Section 2 — What personal data is processed

- [ ] Categories of personal data (identifiers, identity documents, financial info, transaction history, location, IP, device info, communications, on-chain activity).
- [ ] Sources (collected directly / collected automatically / from third parties).
- [ ] **GDPR Art. 13(1)(c) + Art. 14(1)(d)**: purposes + lawful basis (cross-reference DPIA-lite section 3).
- [ ] **LGPD Art. 9, I + II**: specific purpose + categories of data.
- [ ] **CCPA / CPRA §1798.100(a)**: categories collected + sources + purposes + categories sold or shared + retention.
- [ ] **SPI (CPRA)**: sensitive PI category + purpose-of-use disclosure.

### Section 3 — Lawful basis (GDPR + LGPD)

- [ ] For each processing activity: the basis being relied on (Art. 6 / Art. 7 / Art. 9 / Art. 11).
- [ ] If relying on legitimate interest: brief explanation of the interest (GDPR Art. 13(1)(d)).
- [ ] If relying on consent: how to withdraw (GDPR Art. 7(3) / LGPD Art. 8, §5º).

### Section 4 — Purposes of processing

- [ ] Enumerated purposes (account access, KYC compliance, transaction processing, security monitoring, product analytics, marketing, customer support, legal compliance, sanctions screening).
- [ ] Each purpose mapped to data categories used.

### Section 5 — How long data is retained

- [ ] Retention period per category (specific or based on criteria).
- [ ] Deletion mechanism + cascade to sub-processors.
- [ ] Special retention rules (AML records: typically 5 years; tax records: typically 5-7 years; per jurisdiction).
- [ ] **GDPR Art. 13(2)(a) / 14(2)(a); LGPD Art. 15.**

### Section 6 — Sub-processors + Sharing

- [ ] Categories of sub-processors (cloud, identity verification, analytics, marketing, payment, on-chain infrastructure).
- [ ] Specific sub-processors (named list — preferred; or category list with right-to-request specifics).
- [ ] Sharing with regulators / law enforcement under legal obligation.
- [ ] **CCPA "sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising"** — distinct from "sale"; both require disclosure + opt-out.
- [ ] Sub-processor data-processing agreements (DPAs) in place — operational, not user-facing, but referenced.

### Section 7 — Cross-border transfers

- [ ] Countries data is transferred to.
- [ ] Transfer mechanism per recipient country:
  - GDPR Chapter V — adequacy decision; SCCs; BCRs; Art. 49 derogations.
  - LGPD Arts. 33-36 — adequacy; SCCs; specific guarantees; certification; norms; consent.
  - **EU-US Data Privacy Framework** (where US recipients participate; verify current status).
- [ ] Onward-transfer obligations.
- [ ] **GDPR Art. 13(1)(f) + 14(1)(f); LGPD Art. 33-36.**

### Section 8 — Data-subject rights

For each right, explain how to exercise:

- Confirmation of processing (GDPR Art. 15(1) / LGPD Art. 18, I / CCPA "right to know")
- Access (GDPR Art. 15 / LGPD Art. 18, II / CCPA "right to know" specific pieces)
- Rectification (GDPR Art. 16 / LGPD Art. 18, III / CPRA "right to correct")
- Erasure (GDPR Art. 17 / LGPD Art. 18, VI / CCPA "right to delete")
- Restriction (GDPR Art. 18)
- Portability (GDPR Art. 20 / LGPD Art. 18, V / CPRA "right of portability")
- Objection (GDPR Art. 21 / LGPD Art. 18, §1º)
- Automated decision-making review (GDPR Art. 22 / LGPD Art. 20)
- Opt-out of sale / sharing (CCPA / CPRA §1798.120, §1798.121)
- Right to limit use of SPI (CPRA §1798.121)

Include:

- Response timelines per regime.
- How user submits a request (email / portal / form).
- Verification mechanism for identity.
- Right to complain to supervisory authority (GDPR Art. 13(2)(d); LGPD Art. 18, IX; CCPA — complaint to AG / CPPA).

### Section 9 — Security measures (high level)

- [ ] Brief description of technical + organisational measures (encryption, access controls, training).
- [ ] No need to disclose specifics that would compromise security.
- [ ] **GDPR Art. 32; LGPD Art. 46.**

### Section 10 — Breach notification (high level)

- [ ] Commitment to notify per applicable law (GDPR Art. 33/34; LGPD Art. 48; US state breach laws).
- [ ] How notification will be delivered (email, in-app, public notice for high-risk breaches).

### Section 11 — Children's data

- [ ] Age minimum for service.
- [ ] If service is not for children: clear statement + non-collection commitment.
- [ ] If service is for children: COPPA-compliant parental consent process (US); GDPR Art. 8 (EU — 16 default, member-state variable); LGPD Art. 14 (BR — parental consent for under-12; specific consent for 12-18).

### Section 12 — Cookies + Tracking

- [ ] Cookies + similar technologies disclosure.
- [ ] Categories (strictly necessary, preferences, analytics, marketing).
- [ ] Cookie consent mechanism (EU ePrivacy Directive 2002/58/EC requires opt-in for non-essential).
- [ ] Do Not Track signal handling.
- [ ] Global Privacy Control (GPC) honoring (CCPA / CPRA).
- [ ] Cross-reference separate Cookie Policy if used.

### Section 13 — Marketing communications

- [ ] Marketing opt-in mechanism.
- [ ] Unsubscribe mechanism (one-click).
- [ ] CAN-SPAM Act compliance (US); GDPR consent + Art. 21 right to object (EU); LGPD consent (BR).

### Section 14 — On-chain considerations

- [ ] If any personal data goes on-chain: what + why + immutability disclosure.
- [ ] Erasure-right reconciliation explanation (off-chain deletion + on-chain reference; functional encryption; etc.).
- [ ] Acknowledgment of on-chain public visibility (wallet address + transaction history).
- [ ] User assumption of on-chain visibility risk.
- [ ] **Reference [`../domains/privacy-data-protection.md` §On-chain reconciliation](../domains/privacy-data-protection.md).**

### Section 15 — California-specific addendum

- [ ] CCPA / CPRA notice at collection.
- [ ] Categories collected + sold + shared in last 12 months.
- [ ] Sources + purposes.
- [ ] Recipients (sale + sharing + business purposes).
- [ ] Right to opt-out (link visible on every page where applicable).
- [ ] Right to limit use of SPI (link visible where SPI is used beyond limited purposes).
- [ ] Notice of financial incentive (if applicable).
- [ ] Notice of right to non-discrimination.
- [ ] Other-state addenda where applicable (VA / CO / CT / UT / TX / DE / OR / IN / IA / NH / etc. — per current state-law coverage).

### Section 16 — Brazil-specific addendum

- [ ] LGPD-specific notices (encarregado contact; ANPD complaint pathway).
- [ ] PT-BR translation (recommended; required for BR-targeted services).
- [ ] BCB Resoluções 519/520/521 customer-protection-related disclosures (for PSAVs).

### Section 17 — EU-specific addendum

- [ ] EU representative contact (Art. 27).
- [ ] Supervisory authority complaint pathway.
- [ ] If profiling: explicit Art. 22 disclosure + logic-involved description.

### Section 18 — Modification + Effective Date

- [ ] Modification mechanism.
- [ ] Effective date.
- [ ] Version history (preferred).
- [ ] How user is notified of material changes.

### Section 19 — Contact

- [ ] Email / form / mailing address for privacy inquiries.
